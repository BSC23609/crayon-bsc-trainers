Three rules for accurate data:

  1. Ask for a specific answer. Offer the options ("dealer, fabricator or contractor?") instead of open questions.
  2. Say names in full. Product, size, grade, brand and town, never "that one" or "same as last time".
  3. Repeat the answers back before ending the call (5.1). The recording then holds the confirmed facts in one place, which the AI reads most accurately.
